|-----------|---------|

# Must Memorize (SnowPro)

- **USAGE** → Access/use an object.
- **SELECT** → Read data.
- **MODIFY** → Change operational settings (especially Warehouse).
- **MONITOR** → View status/history.
- **OPERATE** → Start/stop operational objects like Tasks/Pipes.
- **READ/WRITE** → Internal Stages.
- **APPLY** → Policies and Tags.
- **OWNERSHIP** → Full control + Grant privileges.
